# Installing the app

You can add OCI to your phone's home screen or your computer's desktop. It then
opens in its own window, without the browser's address bar and tabs, with an
icon, a name and colours chosen by your institution.

An installed OCI is the same website, not a separate program. Everything works
as it does in the browser, and updates arrive the way they do for any web page:
the next time you open it.

## It needs a network connection

OCI has no offline mode. Your conversations are kept on the server, so a copy
on your device would be out of date, and it would be a copy of your private
conversations left on a phone. With no connection the installed app shows the
same error page your browser would show for any site, and nothing from OCI is
kept to show instead. Open it again once you are back online.

## How to install

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| Chrome or Edge on Android | **Settings** → **Customization** → **Install**, or the browser menu → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**). |
| Chrome or Edge on a computer | **Settings** → **Customization** → **Install**, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar. |
| Safari on iPhone or iPad | **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**. |
| Safari on a Mac | **File** → **Add to Dock**. |
| Firefox | Firefox on a computer cannot install websites. On Android, the menu → **Install**. |

The **Install** button in **Settings** → **Customization** appears only when
your browser has said it can install the app. Safari never says so, which is
why iPhones, iPads and Macs use the steps above. If you have already installed
the app, or your browser cannot, the button is not shown.

## Good to know

- **Name and icon.** They are your instance's name, short name and logo, as
  your administrator set them. Under the icon, phones show the short name, cut to 12 characters. Android and
  Chrome notice a change when they next check; on an iPhone or iPad the name
  and icon are copied when you add the app, so remove it and add it again to
  pick up a change.
- **Signing in.** An app added on an iPhone or iPad may keep its own sign-in,
  separate from Safari's, so you may need to sign in once there.
- **Removing it.** Press and hold the icon, or use the app's own menu, and
  choose to remove or uninstall it. Your conversations are not affected.
