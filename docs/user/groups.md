# Groups

A group is a set of people your institution has put together, such as a
department or a course. Being in a group can give you a model or a usage limit
that others do not have. It never takes anything away from you.

You do not need to do anything to be in a group: an administrator or a group
manager adds you. Your usage limits keep their usual wording ("You have reached
your message limit for today"); they do not name the group.

## If you manage a group

A manager decides who is in the group. If you manage at least one group,
**Settings → Groups** appears (it does not appear for anyone else). It lists
the groups you manage, with how many people are in each and how many more the
group can hold (a group has at most 5,000 members).

You can:

- **See who is in the group**, with names, email addresses and whether each is a
  member or a manager. Search by name or email.
- **Add people** by email address: up to 200 at a time. Each address ends up
  in one of three lists: added, already in the group, or no account with that
  address. Only people who already have an account can be added. People you add
  are members, not managers.
- **Remove a member.** It takes effect straight away: the models and limits the
  group gave them stop applying on their next message.
- **Leave** the group yourself. You then stop managing it.

You cannot see what the group grants (its budgets and models) or its usage,
rename or delete the group, or make someone a manager or remove a manager. Ask
an administrator for those. A person who is in no group and manages none cannot
find any of these pages, and a manager of one group cannot look at another.

Looking up addresses is limited to 30 requests a minute, to stop guessing who has
an account.

Every change you make is recorded for administrators, with your name.

## Questions

**I added someone and they cannot use a model the group has.** They get it on
their next request; ask them to reload. If it still does not show, the group may
not be granted that model: an administrator sets that.

**Can I add someone who has no account?** No. Ask them to sign in once, or ask
an administrator to invite them.

**Can I make a second manager?** No. Administrators appoint managers, up to 20 a
group.
